TRANSYLVANIAN BOOK FESTIVAL IN RICHIS

September
Photo by Tessa Traeger
Patchwork by Lilian Theil

Once again MET was pleased to be supporting the Book Festival in Richis, which began on 13th September. MET guesthouses in the surrounding villages were provided for participants - including the beautiful, newly restored Richis house in the heart of the village. Details of MET guesthouses can be found on the Experience Transylvania website. The Trust also sponsored artist Lilian Theil, whose patchwork textiles are beautiful and political and unique. Now in her eighties, she lives in Sighisoara.
For full details about the festival and its programme, see: Festival website

LONDON SCHOOLS SYMPHONY ORCHESTRA ROMANIAN TOUR

July
Peter Ash, conductor of the London Schools Symphony Orchestra

The MET was delighted to welcome this remarkable orchestra, which came to Mălâncrav on Tuesday 31st July. The hundred strong group of young players, was lead by their conductor Peter Ash. They spent a day working and playing with village dancers and musicians who were under the guidance of Andrea Rost.

In the evening they gave a concert at the Apafi Manor which included:

- Faure's Pelleas et Melisande Suite Op 80, Conductors Lucas Boardman and Pooja Low
- Debussy's Premiere Rhapsodie, Clarinet Soloist - James Garagnon - Conductor Hugo Williams
- Traditional Saxon Dance Music (arr. John Kirby), Marsch, Jungsachsisch and Neppendorfer Landler II

Conductors - John Nation, Tanner Hatzmann and Archie Young-Lee
